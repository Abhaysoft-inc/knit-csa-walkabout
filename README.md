# KNIT CSA Walkabout

A first-person 3D walkabout of part of the KNIT Sultanpur campus, running in the browser with three.js.

**Live:** https://abhaysoft-inc.github.io/knit-csa-walkabout/

What's in it:
- CSA (Council of Student Activities) block and the Academic block, with the stair core in the gap between them
- KNIT Alumni Park and the Administrative Block
- Campus roads traced from Google Maps

The buildings were modelled in Blender from Street View photos and exported to `knit-csa.glb`. Sizes and positions are estimates.

## Controls
- **W A S D**: walk · **Mouse**: look · **Shift**: run · **Space**: jump
- **1 to 9**: jump to a place · **N**: day / night · **M**: minimap
- On phones: left pad to walk, drag on the right side to look
